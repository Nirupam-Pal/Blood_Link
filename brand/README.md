# BloodLink Brand Kit

## The idea

A **blood drop** (pointing up) and a **location pin** (pointing down) are the same shape facing opposite ways:
the donor and the person in need. The pin sits in front, joined to the drop by a thin cut-out gap.

> Blood, found where it's needed.

The dot of the **i** in the wordmark is a blood drop, a small signature detail.

**Tagline:** Be the link.

For print, ask the printer to match these to the closest Pantone / CMYK swatch and run a colour proof.
Saturated reds shift a lot between screen and vinyl.

## Rules

- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo at least the height of the pin's hole on every side.
- **Minimum size:** symbol 16 px on screen / 8 mm in print; horizontal logo 80 px / 25 mm wide.
- **Hoarding readability:** the logo should be at least 1/10 of the hoarding height. Headlines of 7 words or fewer.
- **Don't:** recolour the symbol outside the palette, add shadows or gradients, stretch it, outline it,
  rotate it, or place the colour logo on a busy photo (use the white version instead).

## Before sending a hoarding to print

1. Open the SVG in Illustrator, Figma, CorelDRAW, or Inkscape.
2. Replace the headline font with your chosen brand typeface (e.g. Inter, Helvetica Neue, or Geist ExtraBold), then **convert text to outlines**.
3. Export as PDF (CMYK) at the printer's final size; vector stays sharp at any size, with no DPI needed.
4. Add the printer's bleed (usually 2–4 inches on each side for flex and vinyl).
